Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

WRLD Tech Co., a DBA of WRLD Inc. ("WRLD", "we"), operates wrld.tel and the WRLD.Tel business VoIP and AI call analysis service, including the client portal at dash.wrld.tel. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have. It supplements the WRLD Tech Co. privacy policy, which applies across all WRLD properties.

Information we collect

  • Contact and account information: name, business name, email address, phone number and sign-in identity when you contact us or are provisioned as a portal user.
  • Call records and recordings: call metadata (numbers, times, durations, outcomes) and, where recording is enabled by your organisation, the recordings themselves.
  • Derived analysis: transcripts, summaries, sentiment scores, topics and inferred caller details generated from recordings on your organisation's behalf.
  • Usage and technical data: standard server logs, browser type and pages visited on wrld.tel and the portal, used to keep the service secure and working.

How we use information

We use information to provide and support the WRLD.Tel service, to deliver call analysis and dashboards to your organisation, to respond to enquiries, to bill for the service and to protect the platform against abuse. Call recordings and their analysis are processed only for the organisation that owns the account and are never used to train general-purpose AI models.

Who we share it with

Call routing and media are carried by Telzio, our VoIP platform partner. Transcription and analysis run on cloud infrastructure under WRLD's control. Where your organisation enables an integration (for example a CRM sync), confirmed records are sent to the system you connect. We do not sell personal information.

Recording and consent

Your organisation decides whether, where and when calls are recorded, and is responsible for providing any notice or obtaining any consent required in its jurisdiction. WRLD provides the controls to enable recording per line, team or direction and to set retention periods.

Retention and security

Recordings and analysis are retained for the period your organisation configures and deleted afterwards. Access to the portal uses role-based permissions and is logged. Call media is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by contacting helpdesk@wrld.tech. Requests relating to a specific organisation's call data are fulfilled with that organisation's authorisation.
