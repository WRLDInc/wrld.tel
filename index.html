Why WRLD.Tel

A business phone system should cost less, not lock you in.

Most small and mid-sized businesses pay for seats they never use, hardware they never own and contracts they never wanted. WRLD.Tel takes the opposite position on all three.

Save an average of two-thirds on your phone bill

Telzio prices by usage rather than by seat. Businesses switching from traditional per-user VoIP save over 66% on monthly costs on average. We review your current invoice line by line and show you the comparison before you make any decision.

  • Usage-based pricing instead of flat per-seat licences
  • Local, toll-free and international numbers from one account
  • Clear, itemised monthly billing

No contracts, no early termination fees

Service is month-to-month. If WRLD.Tel stops earning its place in your stack, you cancel. There are no penalties, no auto-renewing multi-year terms and no hidden charges on the final invoice.

  • Cancel any month, in writing, with no fee
  • Scale numbers and users up or down as the business changes
  • 60-day money-back guarantee on new accounts

No leased hardware

Use the SIP desk phones you already own, the mobile app on iOS and Android, or a web browser with a headset. If you do want new handsets, we specify and supply them outright, and you own them.

  • Works with standard SIP desk phones and softphones
  • Mobile and desktop apps for remote and hybrid teams
  • No proprietary equipment, no rental line on the bill

Calls are transcribed with speaker separation, then analyzed for sentiment, key topics and action items. You learn how a call went, what was promised and what needs following up, in minutes rather than in a week of spot-checking recordings. Transcripts are searchable, so "the caller who asked about Tuesday's appointment" becomes a two-second lookup.

  • Speaker-separated transcription built for telephony audio, including long calls
  • Sentiment, summary, topics and action items on every recording
  • Searchable call history with filters by number, team member, outcome and date

How it works

From first conversation to first analyzed call in days, not quarters.

Switching phone systems has a reputation for disruption. We plan the cutover so the business keeps answering calls throughout.

  1. We review your bill and call flow

    Share a recent invoice and how calls move through the business today. We return a cost comparison and a proposed call flow: numbers, ring groups, hours, voicemail and overflow.

  2. We build and test the system

    Your WRLD.Tel account is configured on Telzio, apps are provisioned and existing SIP phones are registered. You test inbound and outbound calls on temporary numbers before anything changes.

  3. We port your numbers

    Local and toll-free numbers port across on a scheduled date. Your old service stays live until the port completes, so there is no gap in coverage.

  4. You start learning from calls

    Recordings flow into AI analysis from day one. Within the first week you have transcripts, sentiment and a dashboard view of how the business sounds on the phone.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about business VoIP and AI call analysis.

What is WRLD.Tel?

WRLD.Tel is the business phone service from WRLD Tech Co. It combines cloud VoIP on Telzio's enterprise platform with AI call analysis, a real-time dashboard and Dallas-based support, all on month-to-month terms. It is one of the service branches of wrld.tech, alongside managed IT, hosting, AI automation and design.

How much can we save by switching?

Businesses switching from traditional VoIP or legacy phone systems save an average of two-thirds on monthly costs with Telzio's usage-based pricing. Your result depends on how many seats you pay for today versus how many people actually use the phone. We review your current bill and show you the comparison before you commit to anything.

Do we need to sign a contract or lease hardware?

No. Service is month-to-month with no early termination fees. You can use existing SIP desk phones, the mobile app or a web browser with a headset. There is no proprietary equipment to lease. If you want new handsets we supply them outright.

Can we keep our existing phone numbers?

Yes. Local and toll-free numbers can be ported to WRLD.Tel. We manage the porting process with your current carrier and keep your old service live until the switch completes, so callers never hit a dead line.

How does AI call analysis work?

Recorded calls are transcribed with speaker separation, then analyzed for sentiment, key topics and action items. Results appear in your WRLD.Tel dashboard next to the recording, so you can understand how calls went without listening to every one. Recording can be enabled per line, per team or per direction to match your policy.

Is WRLD.Tel suitable for HIPAA-regulated practices?

The underlying Telzio platform supports HIPAA and SOC 2 compliance. We help clinics and practices configure recording, retention and access controls to fit their obligations, and a business associate agreement can be arranged as part of onboarding.

Where is support based, and how do we reach it?

WRLD is headquartered in Dallas, Texas. Support is handled by WRLD.Support, reachable by phone at 469.299.9598, by email at helpdesk@wrld.tech and through the WRLD ticket portal. Guides and documentation live at WRLD.help.

What is the relationship between WRLD.Tel and Telzio?

WRLD is a certified Telzio partner. Telzio provides the carrier-grade VoIP infrastructure. WRLD adds AI call analysis, the dashboard, setup, number porting, integration with the rest of your technology stack and ongoing support.
